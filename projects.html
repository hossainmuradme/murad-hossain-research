PROCESS DEVELOPMENT PROJECT
Industrial engineering Gas transport Vapor recovery Energy economics

Bulk refrigerant vapor recovery and industrial gas-pressure management

305 kg reported average residual vapor per ISO tank
3 plants manufacturing scope
USD 59,600 vapor-compression system evaluated

Scope. Managed industrial gas networks for tube-joining and bulk storage of R600a (isobutane) and R134a across three manufacturing plants. The project assessed residual vapor left in supplier ISO tanks after pressure equalization, including supplied examples of 330 kg for tank GTPU-5000135 and 280 kg for tank LBTU-8240027.

Engineering context. Bulk reservoirs and 450 kg backup cylinders are reported at 4–6 kg/cm², while main supply lines are boosted to 18–20 kg/cm² and production-floor supply is maintained around 14–16 kg/cm². High-pressure nitrogen is also used for TIG-welding shielding.

Assessment. A vapor-compression recovery system quoted at BDT 7,450,000 was evaluated, and an in-house recovery-machine concept was also explored. The preliminary payback was considered too long to justify proceeding with the project.

Calculation note. The supplied narrative and calculation sheet differ on annual ISO-tank deliveries, annual savings, and payback period. These assumptions should be reconciled before presenting a final savings or payback claim.
