How I approach a problem

From the first measurement to a defensible conclusion.

The exact tools change from project to project; the discipline does not.

01 · QUESTION

Define

State the failure mode, design goal, or performance gap.

02 · SETUP

Instrument

Choose sensors, boundary conditions, and a repeatable protocol.

03 · EVIDENCE

Collect

Record thermal, electrical, flow, or structural responses.

04 · ANALYSIS

Compare

Use plots, statistical bands, coefficient trends, and stress fields.

05 · CHECK

Qualify

Identify limits, verify results, and plan the next test.

Current research directions

Questions I want to pursue further.

These are graduate-level directions that connect naturally to my current experience while leaving room to grow into deeper research.

Thermal signatures for rapid diagnostics

How can full-field thermal images and sparse contact measurements be combined to distinguish fault types earlier, with interpretable uncertainty and robust performance across refrigerator models?

Experimental validation of numerical models

How can mesh sensitivity, transition modelling, and independent validation improve confidence in low-Reynolds-number CFD predictions and geometry-driven performance claims?

Manufacturing-aware lightweight structures

How can topology optimization be connected to practical design-for-manufacturing constraints while preserving strength, stiffness, and production feasibility?

Energy and process efficiency

How can measurement and process data support faster end-of-line tests, lower energy use, and reliable quality decisions at industrial scale?

Research summaries on this site are intentionally concise. Full technical detail, unpublished figures, and industrial evidence should only be shared where co-author, employer, and data permissions allow.

2026 Manuscript in preparation

FEA-Driven Topology Optimization for Lightweight Structural Design: A Computational Framework with Implicit Geometry Reconstruction

Authors: Afsar Mahmud, Murad Hossain, and Hridoy Bhadra.

International Conference on Mechanical, Industrial and Materials Engineering (ICMIME 2026); manuscript status to be confirmed.

A manufacturing-aware computational workflow combines multi-load finite-element analysis, density-based topology optimization, implicit geometry reconstruction, and design-for-manufacturing constraints for a Zamak 3 refrigerator hinge bracket. The supplied draft reports a 45.7% mass reduction, from 446 g to 242 g, with a minimum safety factor of 1.974 for the analysed static load case.

Rapid end-of-line fault detection in household refrigerators from three-point refrigerant-line temperature and compressor electrical profiles within a 15-minute test window

Authors: Murad Hossain and Mehedi Hasan.

Working manuscript; submission and publication status not yet confirmed.

The study evaluates discharge, filter-drier outlet, and suction temperatures alongside compressor electrical signals. In the supplied single-model dataset, a combined temperature-and-power rule flagged 18 of 19 faulty runs with no false alarms among nine normal units; independent replication is still needed.

Rapid Non-Destructive Fault Detection of Domestic Refrigerators Using Infrared Thermography for Industrial Quality Control

Authors: Murad Hossain, Mehedi Hasan, Turja Das Pranta, and Md Raihan Islam.

Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology; manuscript in preparation.

An experimental study uses a FLIR C3 infrared camera to map thermal patterns on refrigerator compartments and refrigeration components. The draft describes a 0.6 m camera stand-off, emissivity set to 0.90, and quantitative temperature extraction from selected surface regions.

2026 Manuscript draft · verification pending

Effect of trailing-edge inward dimples on the aerodynamic performance of an inverted low-Reynolds-number BE50 airfoil: a CFD study

Authors: Murad Hossain, Prashanta Saha, and Md. Emdadul Hoque.

Department of Mechanical Engineering, Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh.

A steady two-dimensional CFD study examines one to three shallow inward dimples near the trailing edge of an inverted BE50 airfoil at a chord Reynolds number of approximately 1.64 × 10⁵. The draft reports angle-dependent drag and lift-to-drag trends. Grid independence, independent validation, and several coefficient entries still require verification before submission.

Simulation of Modified Flipped Airfoil and Analysis of Lift and Drag Coefficient

Authors: Murad Hossain and Prashanta Saha. Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Emdadul Hoque.

BSc thesis, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET).
